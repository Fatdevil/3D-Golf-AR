# Credits

## Grass textures (`grass-tile.jpg`, `grass-tile-2.jpg`)

Derived from vector artwork downloaded from Magnific (formerly Freepik), made into small seamless greyscale tiles.

- `grass-tile-2.jpg`: "Fresh green grass turf on light green background" by **starline**, https://www.magnific.com/free-vector/fresh-green-grass-turf-light-green-background_427670624.htm (free licence: attribution required). Rebuilt as a seamless tile (the middle seam of the first version is gone): crop of the render, low frequencies removed, min-cut wrap seams in x and y.
- `grass-tile.jpg`: made from a soft cartoon-style top-down grass image supplied by the project owner (2026-10, replaces an earlier cartoon tile), scaled, flattened of its large-scale shading and made seamless with a min-cut seam; original source and licence terms to be confirmed and added here. (Before that it came from the vector file `2003.i305.019 realistic green grass`, Magnific / Freepik.)
