# Credits

## Grass textures (`grass-tile.jpg`, `grass-tile-2.jpg`)

`grass-tile-2.jpg` is derived from vector artwork downloaded from Magnific (formerly Freepik); `grass-tile.jpg` is the project owner's own image (see below). Both are made into small seamless greyscale tiles.

- `grass-tile-2.jpg`: "Fresh green grass turf on light green background" by **starline**, https://www.magnific.com/free-vector/fresh-green-grass-turf-light-green-background_427670624.htm (free licence: attribution required). Rebuilt as a seamless tile (the middle seam of the first version is gone): crop of the render, low frequencies removed, min-cut wrap seams in x and y.
- `grass-tile.jpg`: made from a soft cartoon-style top-down grass image that the project owner made themselves with ChatGPT (OpenAI image generation, 2026-10; the owner holds and can document the rights). Scaled, flattened of its large-scale shading, contrast lowered and made seamless with a min-cut seam. No attribution is required.
